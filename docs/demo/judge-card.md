# CASSATION — you are the court

<!-- Print one per judge. Large type: body ≥ 18 pt, the three verbs ≥ 28 pt. -->

You're about to watch AI agents put a research question on trial — and the court
answers to you. You get two moments of real power: before any agent runs, you cut
whatever part of the plan you like, and when the agents deadlock, you decide.
Nothing is scripted around you — what you strike provably never runs, and your
ruling is stamped into the final brief.

## 1. STRIKE

When the plan appears, **tap any claim row to strike it.** Any one you like.
It greys out — no agent will ever run on it.

## 2. APPROVE

**Tap Approve.** The agents launch on what's left.

## 3. RULE

When the court deadlocks, two sourced quotes appear side by side.
**Pick the one you find more credible.** Your ruling is binding — the verdict
will carry it, stamped "human-adjudicated".

---

*Check the final brief: the claim you struck is absent. That's how you know this
was live.*
