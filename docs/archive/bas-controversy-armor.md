# c-BAs Thermal-Conductivity Controversy — Q&A Armor

Factual armor for the demo question. Every row below was verified against the actual
source (or its publisher/OSTI/PMC record) on 2026-10-31. Memorize the number chain:

> **2200 predicted (2013) → 190 measured (2015) → 1400 re-predicted (2017) → 1300/1000/900 measured (2018, Science trio) → ~1500 (2024/25 high-purity & isotope-enriched) → >2100 (Nov 2025, purified arsenic).**

The controversy is *live right now*: the November 2025 Materials Today paper claims
BAs **above 2100 W/mK**, i.e. above the four-phonon theoretical ceiling of ~1400
that the 2018 "confirmation" papers were celebrated for matching. This is better
demo material than the plan assumed — see PLAN CORRECTION.

## ⚠ PLAN CORRECTION (vs CASSATION-PLAN.md)

1. **§4 "~1300 isotope-pure revisions" is wrong on two counts.**
   ~1300 W/mK is the 2018 **natural-abundance** headline measurement (Kang et al.,
   Science). Isotope enrichment gives ~**1500** W/mK (c-¹¹BAs, Adv. Science 2025),
   and the isotope effect in BAs is famously **weak** (8–13 % measured, vs 45 %
   in diamond / ~80 % in c-BN). The big 2025 revision is **upward and chemical**,
   not isotopic: **>2100 W/mK from arsenic-purified crystals** (Materials Today,
   Nov 2025).
2. **§7 demo script (1:25–1:45) "the Skeptic surfaced the 2025 isotope-pure
   measurement contradicting the older headline number" — right year, wrong
   mechanism, wrong direction.** The real 2025 contradiction: the purified-As
   measurement (>2100) sits **above** the old headline (~1300) *and* above the
   four-phonon intrinsic ceiling (~1400). The jury beat still works — Oskar rules
   for the newer measurement — but say "purified-arsenic", never "isotope-pure",
   or a DeepMind judge who knows the field will catch it.
3. §2/§4 "~2200 W/mK predicted" is **correct** (2240 natural / 3170 isotope-pure,
   three-phonon theory).

## 2. What the Skeptic should find

Ranked by demo value. (A) is the DISPUTED-takeover pair.

**A. The headline flip — 2018 record vs 2025 revision (the demo beat).**
- Left card: Kang et al., Science 2018 — κ ≈ **1300 W/mK**, "the highest among all
  metals and semiconductors", widely reported as *confirming* the (revised) theory.
- Right card: Niyikiza et al., Materials Today 2025 — κ **> 2100 W/mK** at room
  temperature in arsenic-purified crystals, i.e. the 2018 "record" was ~60 % low
  because of impurities (Si, C, O), not physics.
- The jury question writes itself: *which number gates the GPU run?*

**B. Theory vs theory: the four-phonon ceiling is breached.**
- Lindsay/Broido/Reinecke 2013: >2000 possible (3-phonon).
- Feng/Lindsay/Ruan 2017: four-phonon scattering caps intrinsic κ at ~**1400** —
  this was the celebrated resolution in 2018.
- Niyikiza 2025: measured **>2100**, *above* that ceiling; their own fits need
  weaker-than-computed 3-phonon rates. The 2017 "correction" now looks like an
  over-correction, and first-principles theory is being re-benchmarked.

**C. The missing isotope dividend.**
- 3-phonon theory promised ~+40 % for isotope-pure BAs (2240 → 3170).
- Measured: **8–13 %** (Adv. Sci. 2025: 1320 → 1500). The weak isotope effect is
  itself evidence that four-phonon scattering is strong — which sits awkwardly
  next to B. A subtle, real, two-sided tension.

**D. A *resolvable* non-dispute (good for showing the system doesn't cry wolf):**
early 190–350 W/mK measurements (2015–2018 Jan) vs the 2018+ values. Not a true
controversy — sample quality (antisite pairs, As vacancies) — and the Advocate/
Clerk should resolve it as *superseded*, not contested.

**E. The "beats diamond" framing.** Press says BAs now "surpasses diamond"
(~2000–2200 for natural diamond), but isotope-enriched diamond exceeds 3000.
Contested superlative; depends which diamond.

## 3. Q&A armor — 10 hard judge questions

1. **Why was BAs predicted to be so conductive in the first place?**
   The heavy-As/light-B mass ratio opens a large gap between acoustic and optic
   phonon branches and bunches the acoustic branches together, which annihilates
   the phase space for three-phonon scattering. With the usual decay channels
   selection-ruled away, predicted κ rivaled diamond (PRL 2013).

2. **Why did the first measurements come in 10× low?**
   Crystal quality, not theory: the 2015 Houston crystals were riddled with
   As–B antisite pairs and As vacancies from CVT growth, giving ~190 W/mK. Every
   improvement in growth since has moved the number up — 351 (2018), ~1000–1300
   (2018 trio), ~1500 (2024), >2100 (2025).

3. **What changed between the 2200 and 1400 predictions?**
   Feng, Lindsay and Ruan (PRB 2017) showed that precisely *because* three-phonon
   scattering is selection-ruled weak in BAs, normally-negligible four-phonon
   processes dominate, cutting predicted κ from ~2200 to ~1400. In 2018 that
   looked like the final word, since experiment landed at 1300.

4. **If four-phonon theory caps BAs at 1400, how is >2100 possible in 2025?**
   That's the open dispute — exactly what Cassation is built to surface. The
   Materials Today paper attributes the gain to purified arsenic (fewer Si/C/O
   impurities) and can only fit its data with *weaker-than-computed* three-phonon
   rates alongside strong four-phonon (hence the steep T⁻¹·⁸). The theory is
   being re-benchmarked right now; nobody has the reconciled answer yet.

5. **Is the >2100 number trustworthy, or one group's claim?**
   Honest status: the crystals all come from one lineage (Ren's group, Houston),
   so it is not yet independently *grown*-replicated. But it is corroborated by a
   second measurement technique (transducer-less ns-TDTR, Research 2025, >2000),
   and David Broido — author of the original prediction — is a co-author. Treat
   it as strong-but-young: precisely CONTESTED, not SUPPORTED.

6. **Why is the isotope effect tiny in BAs when diamond and c-BN gain 45–80 %?**
   Arsenic is naturally monoisotopic and, being heavy, dominates the
   heat-carrying acoustic modes; boron's isotope mix mostly perturbs optic modes.
   And where isotope scattering would matter, four-phonon scattering already
   dominates. Measured enhancement: 8–13 % (Adv. Sci. 2025).

7. **Are these local or bulk numbers?**
   Mostly local: TDTR probes a ~10 µm spot, and BAs crystals are spatially
   heterogeneous — the 2025 transducer-less study maps 1500→2400 W/mK across one
   crystal, and Tian 2018 reported ~1000 local vs ~900 bulk. Any "record" quote
   needs the spot-size caveat; that caveat is on the claim card.

8. **Aren't the three 2018 Science papers just one result?**
   No — three independent groups, growth processes and metrology chains: UCLA
   (Kang/Hu) ~1300, UT Dallas+UIUC (Li/Lv/Cahill) 1000 ± 90, Houston+MIT
   (Tian/Ren/Chen) ~900–1000. They corroborate the order of magnitude and
   disagree in detail — the spread is defect density, which is the 2025 story
   in embryo.

9. **Which number should gate Oskar's GPU run?**
   Depends on the decision: for *as-growable material today*, the defensible
   number is 1000–1500 (defect-limited, multiply replicated). For the *intrinsic
   ceiling* in a device model, the honest answer is "contested: 1400 (theory) vs
   >2100 (newest measurement)". The verdict brief should carry both, with
   provenance — that two-number answer IS the product's value.

10. **Why does the exact value matter — isn't 1000+ already great?**
    Because BAs is a *semiconductor* (diamond is a wide-gap insulator that's hard
    to dope), with high predicted and measured ambipolar mobility (Science 2022
    pair) and a reasonable match to Si/GaAs — so it's a candidate for cooling
    substrates integrated with electronics. Whether its ceiling is 1400 or 2400
    changes whether it merely matches copper-diamond composites or genuinely
    replaces diamond heat spreaders — a fab-investment-sized difference.

## 4. Honest-uncertainty notes (where the literature genuinely disagrees)

- **The intrinsic κ of defect-free BAs is unsettled.** Four-phonon first-principles
  says ~1400; the best 2025 measurement says >2100 with fits requiring modified
  three-phonon rates. No published first-principles reconciliation yet (as of
  2026-10-31). If a judge asks "so what IS the number?", the correct answer is
  "above 2100 experimentally, and theory is catching up."
- **No independent crystal-growth replication of >2000.** All >1500 crystals trace
  to the Houston (Ren) growth line; the independent corroboration so far is by
  *technique* (tl-TDTR) and by partner labs (UCSB, UC Riverside), not by an
  unrelated grower.
- **Even the best samples are still defect-limited.** Hou et al. (PRB 2025) find
  defect spectroscopic signatures and anomalously steep T-dependence even in
  champion crystals — so >2100 may itself be a floor, not the ceiling.
- **Temperature exponent disagreement:** κ ∝ T⁻¹·⁸ (Niyikiza 2025) / ~T⁻²
  (Hou 2025) vs the ~T⁻¹ expected from pure 3-phonon — size of 4-phonon
  contribution still argued.
- **Isotope-effect magnitude varies by report:** 8–13 % (Adv. Sci. 2025
  measurements) up to ~20–30 % claimed elsewhere; depends on baseline sample
  purity, since impurity scattering masks isotope scattering.
- **Measurement uncertainty bars are rarely quoted in press coverage.** Kang 2018
  is "~1300" (we did not pin the published ±); Li 2018 is 1000 ± 90; seeded-growth
  is 351 ± 21; Kim 2016 is 186 ± 46. Use the ± forms where we have them.
